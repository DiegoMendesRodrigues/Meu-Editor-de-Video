## Nome do produto

Meu Editor de Vídeo

> O nome reservado é o mesmo em todos os idiomas; não traduza.

## Descrição (até 10.000 caracteres)

```text
Meu Editor de Vídeo is a complete video editor for Windows 11 that does everything on your computer, including artificial intelligence. Your videos, photos, audio and projects are never sent to the internet.

EDIT FREELY
Build your video on a timeline with multiple video, image, audio, text and caption tracks. Cut, split, join cuts, move and trim clips with frame accuracy, use snapping and ripple editing, mark important points and undo any action from the history. Adjustment layers, compound clips, split screen and multicam help with larger projects.

IMPORT ALMOST ANYTHING
MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI and many other video formats; MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC, OGG and Opus audio; JPG, PNG, WebP, TIFF and SVG images; animated GIFs and image sequences. Lightweight proxies keep editing smooth even with 4K video.

RECORD YOUR SCREEN AND WEBCAM
Record the screen, webcam and microphone at the same time, choose the monitor, place the webcam in a corner and stop with F10.

EFFECTS, COLOR AND COMPOSITING
Transitions, color adjustments, curves, color wheels, LUTs, HSL and color matching between clips; blur, chroma key, animated masks, motion tracking, stabilization, lens correction and noise reduction. Slow motion with frame interpolation, speed ramps, reverse playback, keyframes and 2.5D compositing with perspective.

TEXT AND CAPTIONS
Titles, credits, lower thirds and animated text with outline and shadow. Import SRT and VTT captions, edit them in the caption editor and export them as files or burned into the video.

GREAT AUDIO
Mixer with meters, volume keyframes, fades, equalizer, compressor, loudness normalization (EBU R128), automatic music ducking under speech, voice changer, beat markers and audio-based clip sync.

LOCAL ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
• Remove the background behind people without a green screen (model included).
• Generate captions automatically from speech.
• Isolate voices and reduce noise.
• Remove silent parts and detect scene changes.
• Sharpen images, retouch and blur faces.
• Automatically reframe to 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5, following the person.
Optional models are downloaded once, from their authors' official sites, only when you ask. After that, everything works offline. Uses your graphics card (DirectML) when available.

VIRTUAL ASSISTANT
Ask questions about the program in your language (Shift+F1). Answers come from the user manual, offline, and point to the source section.

EXPORT ANYWHERE
Ready-made export templates for YouTube, Shorts, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp and Facebook, or set everything yourself: format, codec (H.264, H.265, AV1, ProRes and more), resolution, frame rate and quality. Hardware-accelerated encoding on NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics, a render queue and background export.

SIX LANGUAGES
Interface and manual in English, Brazilian Portuguese, European Portuguese, Spanish, French and German.

SAFE AND PRIVATE
No account, no ads and no telemetry. Autosave and project recovery after an unexpected shutdown.

ALSO FOR LINUX AND MACOS
Meu Editor de Vídeo is also available for free on Linux (Debian and Ubuntu) and macOS, from the official website.

Free software, licensed under the GNU GPL version 3 or later.
```
